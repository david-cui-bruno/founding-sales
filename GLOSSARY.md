# Callie founder sales

Callie keeps business relationships, their conversation evidence and the work needed to advance them. Recording a relationship or conversation does not authorize outreach.

## Language

**Firm**:
A business organization whose identity and business context are tracked in Callie.
_Avoid_: account when referring to an organization

**Person**:
A human business correspondent whose identity exists independently of any known firm relationship.
_Avoid_: contact when referring to the independent human identity

**Contact**:
A person's firm-specific operational association used by existing contact and outreach workflows.
_Avoid_: person when referring to an operational association

**Endpoint**:
An observed email address or phone number associated with a person or firm; it may be shared or change hands.
_Avoid_: identity when referring only to an address or number

**Route**:
A firm/contact-associated endpoint evaluated for use by an outreach channel under its current policy.
_Avoid_: permission when referring only to a reachable endpoint

**Calling preparation**:
An unqualified company record whose reviewed context and phone depend on a current retained source and explicit kept choice. It conveys no qualification or contact permission.
_Avoid_: prepared prospect when referring only to this source-dependent context

**Ideal customer profile (ICP)**:
A testable description of the businesses and operating circumstances in which Callie can deliver value and be adopted successfully. It remains a hypothesis until supported by customer outcomes.
_Avoid_: qualification or contact permission when referring only to commercial fit

**Prospect priority**:
The relative order in which a plausible prospect deserves attention, with its reasons and unknowns. Low priority does not itself mean rejection or ineligibility.
_Avoid_: verified lead or buying probability when referring only to rank

**Buying role**:
A Person's supported or hypothesized responsibility in evaluating, approving, operating or advocating a purchase. A job title alone does not confirm purchasing authority.
_Avoid_: decision-maker when the actual responsibility is unknown

**Buying signal**:
A dated observation that may suggest interest or a reason to consider change. Its relevance to a purchase remains an interpretation; service capability or missing online activity alone does not establish intent.
_Avoid_: confirmed need when referring only to a possible timing clue

**Audio test**:
An explicitly requested call with someone expecting it, used to check two-way audio outside a sales relationship.
_Avoid_: prospect call when referring only to an audio check

**Firm relationship**:
A supported association between a person and a firm, with historical or current context and explicit unknown dates.
_Avoid_: employment when the evidence establishes only an association

**Opportunity**:
A distinct commercial initiative explicitly pursued with a firm; its stage and outcome are separate from other initiatives at that firm.
_Avoid_: conversation, prospect

**Conversation**:
A collection of observed communications whose participants and business context can be known, unresolved or shared across relationships.
_Avoid_: opportunity when referring only to communications

**Source**:
An observed or explicitly selected communication, transcript, note or file that supports an interpretation.
_Avoid_: fact when referring to raw source content

**Claim**:
A stated or inferred interpretation tied to source evidence; it may remain uncertain or conflict with another claim.
_Avoid_: confirmed fact when no human confirmation exists

**Human decision**:
An explicit confirmation, dismissal or correction of a particular claim or association.
_Avoid_: model confidence when referring to human confirmation

**Commitment**:
A supported promise to perform an action, distinguished from a suggested next step or an ambiguous expression of interest.
_Avoid_: next step when implying the correspondent promised it

**Coverage**:
The known extent and gaps of captured or processed conversation history within a stated scope.
_Avoid_: completeness when unresolved gaps remain

**Ask**:
The section for finding business records, exact operational state and original passages by keywords. It does not generate answers.
_Avoid_: Ask mode, separate Search section

**Activation**:
Making a capability operational after its required evidence, configuration and release checks pass.
_Avoid_: source completion when referring to operational readiness

**Capability authority receipt**:
A historical record of independent review for a precisely configured capability, owner and scope.
_Avoid_: current owner consent, diagnostic result, sending permission

**Owner consent**:
The owner's explicit approval of a capability's data scope, purpose, provider, model and spending allowance. A change to that scope requires renewed approval.
_Avoid_: provider verification, measured quality, sending permission

**Owner approval audit**:
The retained record of what the owner approved and which limitations were acknowledged.
_Avoid_: technical acceptance result, proof of model usefulness

**Acquisition diagnostic authorization**:
A dated approval to test acquisition of explicitly selected business messages within an isolated environment and a conserved read allowance. It does not authorize production capture, hosted processing or outreach.
_Avoid_: capability authority receipt, capture activation

**Provisional quality**:
A declared level of usefulness supported by limited evaluated evidence, with remaining uncertainty and gaps made visible.
_Avoid_: representative reliability when evidence is limited

**Representative quality**:
Usefulness evaluated against varied business evidence appropriate to the claimed scope, with the categories and limitations disclosed.
_Avoid_: synthetic pass when referring to observed real business performance

**Public thread snapshot**:
A bounded retained observation of a public discussion at a stated time and access context, with explicit coverage gaps and unknown attribution.
_Avoid_: conversation history or current platform state when referring only to a captured excerpt
