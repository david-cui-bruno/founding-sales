# Preserve original conversation context through identity corrections

The approved broader CRM spec #479 and relationship slice #481 preserve each copied source's explicitly selected person/firm context and relationship revision. Correcting a current relationship does not rewrite historical conversation context or operational contact associations. Unknown dates remain unknown; a shared firm endpoint does not identify a speaker. Conflicting, recycled or out-of-date claims require review rather than automatic merging or an arbitrary owner.

Source access requires the copy's owner or exceptional audited administrator access and current authority for every explicit original firm context. Firm-owned copies also require their actual firm's authority. Explicit contexts can replace a person copy's legacy default association; they do not authorize new default-context capture, sending or operational route changes. Content-free deleted/awaiting-recapture identities use their last explicit source-version snapshots for scope and authorization. Explicit recapture preserves those immutable snapshots, adds bounded new-version snapshots marked for review, and does not revalidate deleted relationships or endpoint claims.

A copied conversation involving several contexts is indivisible. A scoped deletion removes the whole affected copy and invalidates dependent evidence, while unrelated supported copies and identity can survive. Preview approval binds body-free evidence versions as well as counts. Identity mutations and deletion collect sorted firm, person and source locks, recheck current authority after waits, and refuse changed dependency closures instead of extending locks out of order. These source decisions preserve the release hold and separate evaluation/activation gates.
