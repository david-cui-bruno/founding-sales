# Separate capability authority from settings

CRM acquisition and hosted processing need a supported activation path while preserving #479's independent evaluation, data-handling and release gates. Use independently reviewed, body-free authority receipts provisioned through trusted operations; normal authenticated settings commands may reference these receipts but cannot create them. Keep application access to the authority registry read-only so configuration or a diagnostic manifest cannot authorize itself.

Receipts bind the exact owner, capability, resulting configuration revision, account and applicable evidence/release versions. Runtime consumers recheck current bindings, expiry and revocation after waits; known provider usage remains financially settled even when publication loses authority. Disabled saves and explicit disable actions clear the current authority association, while immutable command receipts preserve dated actions.

Mailbox acquisition acceptance and representative AI evaluation remain distinct. Actual OAuth observations come from normal provider consent, and existing mailboxes receive no invented scope history. A bounded diagnostic cannot authorize production semantic retrieval, provider processing or outreach. Defaults remain disabled; this decision supplies source contracts and does not lift the release hold or establish live acceptance.

Retain immutable, body-free capability receipts and normal OAuth scope observations as authorization audit history. They contain configuration, workspace owner/reviewer identifiers, provider account identity (which can be the owner email), scopes, dated review/evidence references and revocation status, never copied conversation bodies or provider tokens. Source deletion removes its copies and dependent content without erasing this consent history. Expiry, disconnect, generation change and departure do not delete documentary history; current membership, account, configuration, release, expiry and revocation checks make it unusable for fresh acquisition or processing. The retention worker must not sweep these immutable tables.

## Approved amendment — October 11, 2026

David approved a monitored, David-only initial AI quality tier because representative business conversations are not yet available in sufficient variety. The original decision above remains the historical rationale and the authority model remains in force. This amendment distinguishes provisional usefulness from representative quality; it does not turn a synthetic pass or a Gmail acquisition diagnostic into representative evidence.

**David-only provisional quality** permits preparing narrowly scoped hosted CRM interpretation or Ask use for David when all applicable prerequisites pass: a critical synthetic safety/regression suite, actual model/provider integration, a reviewed labeled evaluation set, and independently approved purpose, data handling, source scope and spend limits. The receipt must state the provisional tier, evaluated capabilities and versions, evidence classes, measured results, missing representative categories, owner and authorized scope. Product status must visibly identify provisional quality and its limitations. Ongoing review must examine actual outputs and corrections, retain failures as evaluation cases and reassess the affected capability as real business evidence accumulates. Critical forbidden exposure, wrong-person attribution, invented definitive state or unsupported definitive answers veto the affected capability; unaffected independently passing capabilities need not be disabled.

**Representative quality** requires evaluation on appropriately varied, authorized real business evidence with reviewed labels and disclosed denominators, categories and limitations. Provisional evidence may be retained as regression evidence, but cannot be relabeled as representative quality. Synthetic scenarios, limited vendor/support examples, provider transport acceptance and representative sales/customer quality remain distinct claims. No fixed number of acquired customer threads is implied by this amendment, and small samples do not establish general reliability.

Both tiers preserve independent trusted review and body-free authority provisioning outside ordinary application settings. Receipts and runtime checks continue to bind exact owner, capability, configuration revision, account/generation where applicable, applicable evidence, deployed commit/image/schema and expiry/revocation. Evidence may be reused only for its reviewed applicable scope; a changed deployment still requires valid exact-release authority. Neither deployment nor an automatically assembled readiness proposal activates a capability by itself.

This amendment grants no outreach, new recipient/enrollment, public rollout or paid-call execution approval. Sending safeguards, independent disable controls and the production release hold remain unchanged. Each actual provider call still needs its applicable purpose/data/spend authorization. Local acquisition acceptance is addressed separately by ADR0010 and remains distinct from hosted processing authority.

The inspected implementation currently requires `actual_representative` evidence for hosted CRM extraction and Ask activation. That guard remains blocking until the approved provisional tier is implemented and verified through the normal source, release and authority paths. Recording this decision establishes neither current readiness nor activation and authorizes no paid evaluation call.
