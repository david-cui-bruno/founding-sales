# 0013: Approved email campaigns admit qualified canonical Firms without per-recipient approval

## Status and scope

Accepted October22 2026 for the already-agreed reversible LOCAL product continuation. This implements desired automatic email admission into already-approved campaigns, superseding the future manual-only restriction in sequences documentation. It grants no production enrollment, sending, acquisition, inference, provider, hosting, data routing or infrastructure activation. ADR0005 commercial/outreach separation, ADR0009 settings/authority separation and ADR0012 browser security/custody remain.

## Decision

A published sequence and prospecting mailbox authorization do not constitute campaign approval. Store an explicit immutable approval binding the current admin owner, mailbox/provider authorization revision, approved five-email sequence, selected TX/RI/MA region subset under existing narrower geography policy, admission lifetime/daily caps and expiry. No target-country/channel expansion. At most one unrevoked campaign per owner, bounded expiration within30days, lifetime at most25 admissions and at most5/day. Revocation prevents future admission and does not erase history or restart stopped/uncertain work. Approval and revocation are standard revision-aware commands with opaque IDs-only receipts.

Reuse current source-backed qualification, canonical identity, business-email association, suppression/reply/refusal, holds, outreach plan and enrollment owners. Email-only qualification does not require a phone number or establish an unmet maintenance need. It still requires current source-supported residential management, target service area, exact unambiguous business email/association and ownership. Keep uncertain cases internal. Do not set a synthetic `reviewed:true` or label automation as a human review. Introduce narrowly verified campaign authority provenance, preserving ordinary manual cohort semantics and source deletion rules.

Approving one campaign considers at most25 current canonical qualified research Firms under the remaining caps. Creating/linking another qualified canonical research Firm invokes the same bounded campaign admission owner automatically, without per-recipient approval. This does not claim automatic acquisition or scheduled provider work. Atomic composition creates/reuses one contact/route, one existing outreach plan, enrollment and first execution with a durable admission identity. A savepoint conserves parent creation on a confirmed admission refusal, while unknown failures remain transactionally honest. All admission paths acquire the shared exclusive send gate before any other locks, preserve current authority after waits, and cannot clear newer stop facts. No other enrollment/sending engine or generic job platform is introduced.

Enrollment is not delivery. Existing route technical validation, coverage, sender/cap/ramp/local window, provider identity, suppression and uncertain-attempt rules remain authoritative at execution. Owner OFF and runtime sending settings are never changed by campaign approval or admission. Short generated openings are a future feature, not part of this exact-template campaign slice. Replies and one-off messages remain final-review-first.

The closed browser extension consists only of private current control read and approve/revoke commands. Pending policy/command custody is per-tab, exact IDs/revisions/UUID, public session binding and expiry only, without addresses, source prose or credentials. Persist before dispatch, never automatically replay or mint a replacement UUID after an unknown result. Preserve pending custody on authenticated content/source unavailability, clear it on real expiry/logout/auth/identity/role/revision change, and fence late responses and cross-tab transitions. Receipts are history, not current proof.
