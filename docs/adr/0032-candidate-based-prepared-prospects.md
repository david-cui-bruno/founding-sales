# Prepare company research independently of publication and contact readiness

David's October30 retained-company audit and implementation instruction prioritize high-volume useful prospects under unchanged budgets and safeguards. The default Prospects view reads candidate-based company briefs from currently permitted retained evidence, independently of canonical Firm publication, native qualification and eligible email. This supersedes ADR0014 only as the entrance to prepared research; ADR0014/0016 publication/campaign authority and ADR0031's existing producer remain unchanged.

The new preparation-only list/read interface produces no business writes, source fetch, model request, paid attempt, canonical association, contact, route or enrollment. Use exact current candidate/revision/latest-run references, existing source/access/retention/deletion checks, current administrator and fresh post-wait expiry. Missing email, phone, buyer, pain, residential fit or precise service geography stays unknown. Attributed business or substantive operational context supports a research brief without one residential-management phrase, a rigid standalone name or same-block city/state service sentence. A search title or query location remains a hint. Source loss, contradictory identity and franchise/office ambiguity cannot be laundered into canonical facts or channel authority.

Reuse existing candidate/priority ordering and bounded pages; complete permitted original passages provide selected detail. Old facts/verdicts/producers, saved human judgments, attempt fingerprints, budgets, pacing, owner OFF, suppression, consent and UNKNOWN custody are not rewritten. The first slice uses complete retained pages; incomplete-page interpretations and future model-instruction changes require their own changed-behavior acceptance. Controlled tests establish the new interface, while actual hosted briefs establish useful output separately after protected delivery.

The ordinary new-input readback found useful maintenance context withheld by the business-only assessment and a directory profile described as first-party company context. Preparation assesses useful research independently by field: substantive operations can support a brief while business/residential fit remains unknown. Contact-only, address-only, bare emergency headings, vendor promotions and testimonials do not independently establish usable operational research; contrary context remains visible and refuses a positive assessment.

A discovery website and same-site capture flag do not independently prove that a company authored the page. Prepared summaries therefore describe attributed retained source passages and disclose unknown publisher/company authority. Source URLs and capture dates remain visible, including directory origins. This changes no source grant or capture flag: complete-page, exact permission/lineage, host, principal, expiry, deletion, contrary and repeated-current-read checks remain. Existing model input, paid fingerprints and strict identity/publication/contact consumers are unchanged. A directory lead or useful partial context is not a verified company or a contact-ready prospect.
