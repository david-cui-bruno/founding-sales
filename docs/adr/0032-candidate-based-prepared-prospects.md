# Prepare company research independently of publication and contact readiness

David's October30 retained-company audit and implementation instruction prioritize high-volume useful prospects under unchanged budgets and safeguards. The default Prospects view reads candidate-based company briefs from currently permitted retained evidence, independently of canonical Firm publication, native qualification and eligible email. This supersedes ADR0014 only as the entrance to prepared research; ADR0014/0016 publication/campaign authority and ADR0031's existing producer remain unchanged.

The new preparation-only list/read interface produces no business writes, source fetch, model request, paid attempt, canonical association, contact, route or enrollment. Use exact current candidate/revision/latest-run references, existing source/access/retention/deletion checks, current administrator and fresh post-wait expiry. Missing email, phone, buyer, pain, residential fit or precise service geography stays unknown. Attributed business or substantive operational context supports a research brief without one residential-management phrase, a rigid standalone name or same-block city/state service sentence. A search title or query location remains a hint. Source loss, contradictory identity and franchise/office ambiguity cannot be laundered into canonical facts or channel authority.

Reuse existing candidate/priority ordering and bounded pages; complete permitted original passages provide selected detail. Old facts/verdicts/producers, saved human judgments, attempt fingerprints, budgets, pacing, owner OFF, suppression, consent and UNKNOWN custody are not rewritten. The original v1 reader uses complete retained pages. The v2 prepared reader also uses intact, hash-bound blocks retained from incomplete pages, with per-passage partial-page attribution and explicit incomplete coverage. Omitted content may qualify or contradict visible passages; unknown fields and absence cannot be established from those omissions. Observed contrary context still refuses a positive preparation assessment, including retained text beyond the display bound. Existing strict model/publication/phone/email completeness rules remain unchanged. Controlled tests establish the new interface, while actual hosted briefs establish useful output separately after protected delivery.

The ordinary new-input readback found useful maintenance context withheld by the business-only assessment and a directory profile described as first-party company context. Preparation assesses useful research independently by field: substantive operations can support a brief while business/residential fit remains unknown. Contact-only, address-only, bare emergency headings, vendor promotions and testimonials do not independently establish usable operational research; contrary context remains visible and refuses a positive assessment.

A discovery website and same-site capture flag do not independently prove that a company authored the page. Prepared summaries therefore describe attributed retained source passages and disclose unknown publisher/company authority. Source URLs and capture dates remain visible, including directory origins. Partial-page interpretation changes no source grant or capture flag: exact permission/lineage, host, whole-block hashes, principal, expiry, deletion, contrary and repeated-current-read checks remain. Strict downstream consumers retain their complete-page requirements. Existing model input, paid fingerprints and strict identity/publication/contact consumers are unchanged. A directory lead or useful partial context is not a verified company or a contact-ready prospect.

The October31 three-record inspection reproduced zero displayed passages: two currently permitted primary pages had useful intact blocks but hit the100-block capture bound; the third record had no currently permitted captured page. V2 separates property-management business and operational fields from residential-fit evidence. Tenant portals, rent payment, screening and marketing services may provide useful operational research without an explicit residential claim; headings, generic education, vendor promotion, testimonials and contact-only text remain insufficient. Capture bounds and whole blocks are preserved; no omitted HTML is reconstructed, no quotations are sliced or joined, and no model, historical qualification or paid attempt is replayed. The source-association policy remains separately versioned in ADR0033.
