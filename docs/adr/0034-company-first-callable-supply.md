# Discover companies, not pages, and measure callable supply

David's October31 step-back agreed that prospect supply should be measured in callable companies, not candidate records. The same-day audit of 24 prepared records found 19 distinct companies, 3 directory or "top N" list pages and 2 repeats of an already counted brand; none had an established named buyer. Discovery turned every search hit into one candidate, so list pages became briefs that mixed passages about unrelated companies, and dictionary, encyclopedia and portal pages consumed intake slots.

Discovery now treats a search hit as a pointer. A hit is classified as a company site, a list or directory page, or a non-company page. A list page is fetched once through the existing research fetcher and its URL, DNS, robots and pause checks; outbound links to other company hosts become discovery leads, with the link text as the discovery label. The list page is never itself a candidate and never a source of facts about the companies it names. Known non-company hosts are dropped. A company host is the lowercase hostname without `www.`; a host already present as a candidate website or source, or as a Firm website, is not created again. The existing per-search new-candidate limit, search quota, research budgets, pacing and paused schedules are unchanged.

Facts about a company come only from that company's own site through the existing capture and qualification path. A company's own-site capture covers its landing page; when the landing page is deeper than its host's root (a search hit's contact or listing page), that host's homepage first, linked or not, because it is where a company says what it does; then, from the landing page's own same-site links, one contact, one about/company and one team/leadership/staff page by path, in that preference order, within the existing page and input bounds; an auxiliary page is left out before a preferred one. Each linked page is requested once through the existing fetcher and its URL, DNS, robots, pause and current-source checks. Under the existing prepared capture policy (v2) its association records the actual lineage: the landing page's traced request chain from the exact candidate source, then the linked page's own chain. Every URL in it keeps its own loss, expiry and denial check, and losing any one erases the association. No other auxiliary page, guessed path or off-site link is associated. The capture requests, model input bound, producer, prompt and paid fingerprint are unchanged, and no migration is involved: the stored manifest shape and reader are those of ADR0033's v2, whose lineage now includes this one published link hop. ADR0031–0033 source grants, expiry, loss, deletion and attribution rules are unchanged. Automatic capture of new discovery leads no longer depends on the strict verified-firm attestation, which continues to gate only strict publication and admission.

A prepared prospect is **callable** when its own-site retained passages include a published phone and attributed property-management business context, with no contrary context. Residential fit and a named person are shown when the retained text supports them. Callable is research readiness, not contact permission: Calling's existing preparation, route, suppression, consent and current-source checks decide any call, and email remains OFF.

Published phones use the same-block literal grammar, version 2. Version 1 spans and refusals are unchanged. Version 2 also accepts a call/phone verb directly followed by one number with ordinary prose after it, and a block containing only one formatted US number with optional leading phone emoji and trailing email. Neither form applies in a block mentioning fax, mobile, cell, emergency, text, SMS, personal, vendor or testimonial wording. A standalone number is its own label: schema154 relaxes the stored choice constraint to allow a span whose label is the literal itself, and nothing else.

Calling shows and keeps the phone that made a prepared company callable. Calling's opted-in read scans, for the candidate's latest qualification run, exactly the pages the prepared brief may cite (one shared admission rule: a current exact source grant or prepared source association, with no capture manifest required), under a `prepared_capture` authority bounded by the earliest source deadline and seven days after retrieval. Intact blocks of incomplete pages count and the read is labelled `partial`; pages the brief would not show are skipped, never promoted. A candidate with no admitted page keeps the exact retained-original rules, the default queue list is unchanged, and no phone-discovery run or grant is needed. A kept prepared-page number is a manual annotation re-read on every list and save. An admin may also save that company for Calling (schema155): the preparation's source is then the candidate's latest qualification run and the exact kept observation, block and hashes (`calling_preparations.source_kind='prepared_capture'`; `discovery_run_ref` holds the qualification run id and the preparer stands as the authority holder, so the saved company needs no phone-discovery run). It carries the number's earliest page deadline as its own expiry and the hashes of every URL it depends on (website, candidate sources, the cited page and each capture hop), so source loss, expiry, a changed or cleared run observation, a withdrawn choice or a newer qualification run withdraws and physically erases it exactly like a lost discovery run. Route, suppression, consent and current-source checks are the same ones, run before anything is created, and nothing places or reserves a call.

Raw discovery places (`discovery-v1`: Overture and the other retained discovery providers) that name an http(s) website, a supported US place and a retrieval date are also due for this own-site research (David approved the roughly 1,801 never-researched Overture places and kept the other retained providers in scope, about one cent a run), through the same scheduler, daily firm ceiling, holds and 7-day refresh as a Tavily draft. The website, read as https, is the landing page; the stored candidate keeps its raw payload, is never kept, admitted or published by this, a place the user skipped is not researched, and one company host gets one research: a place is not due when a kept or Tavily candidate, an already researched place or an earlier place stands for the same host. This narrows only the qualification-scheduling exclusion of ADR0014/0019: publication, monitoring, admission and the legacy listing still exclude `discovery-v1`.

Consequences: multi-location brands collapse to one candidate per host, so separate offices on the same host are not separate prospects in this version. Stored candidates are not rewritten; existing list-page records stay visible as research but are never callable. Step1.2.5's candidate-record yield is superseded by callable companies per day as the supply measure (Step1.6).
