# 0037: The named decision-maker is read from the company's own pages, free; Apollo is retired

## Status and scope

Proposed November 2 2026 for David's approval; amends [ADR0036](0036-named-person-email-enrichment.md). Apollo is not affordable. Research #909 (50 callable-style hosts, own pages read by hand, not by this code) found a named decision-maker on the company's own site for 40 of 50, and a personal-name address published on the site for 20 of 50 (mostly `first@domain`). The campaign still goes to a named owner, president, principal, managing or designated broker or property manager at a personal address; the source of that person is now the company's own retained pages. Nothing here sends, contacts anyone, calls a network or spends a credit; owner sending stays OFF and the campaign's approval, caps, holds and send gate are unchanged.

## Decision

**Data source: the company's own published pages only.** The prepared capture (ADR0033/0034: homepage, contact, about and team pages of the company's host) already retains visible text blocks, and that is all it keeps. `PreparedEmailRead` now carries the admitted pages' blocks (observation, URL, block id, text); the people source reads exactly those. `<script>` and `<title>` are dropped by `parsePageText`, and `mailto:` targets are never read, so **JSON-LD (`founder`, `email`) is not retained and a person published only there is not found** (R1: one host of fifty, `overwatchrem.com`). The capture is unchanged: widening it would change retained hashes and bounds for every prepared company and is not small or safe here. Qualification's model facts (`aws_bedrock.sourcing_qualification`) carry no person or owner fact: roadmap 1.6.3's "named person" is the deterministic passage classifier in `preparedProspectFields.ts`, so this uses deterministic parsing too: no model, no spend, testable on the page shapes below.

**Extraction (`outreach/ownSitePeople.ts`, pure).** A person is a name next to a decision-maker title in one of: `Name, Title` / `Name - Title` / `Name | Title` (team cards, bylines, the TX TREC and CA DRE licence footers `Ryan Stetson, Broker`, `TX License #597044-B Ryan Stetson, Broker`), `Title: Name` / `Designated Broker ~ Name TREC #`, `Name is the Owner`, or a name-only block directly followed by a title-only block (cards). All-capitals lines are read. A dash-led signature (`- Jon Smith, Owner / Broker`) counts, as medium confidence, only with a company-side title (broker, CEO, president, founder, principal, property manager): a bare `Owner` is as likely a customer's. Customer, tenant, client, homeowner, landlord and investor titles, assistants, associates, leasing agents and any line containing testimonial or review words are refused; company names, page labels and initials-only first names are not names. A person with no title is no person.

**An address counts only when the page published it for that person**: in the same block (one address, or the one that matches the name when several), in the next blocks of the same card, or inside the person's own statement. **No address is attached from elsewhere on the page**: a line such as `Email Jane at jane@peak.org` in another block or page (or in a testimonial section) is NOT attributed by first name, because that fallback bypassed the testimonial and own-statement checks; a person without a same-card or own-statement address is found without an email (a later free guess-and-verify step may cover them). Beside another person, or in another block, the address must also carry the person's first name, nickname, last name or a three-letter prefix of the first name (`doc@`, `jlasley@`, `dbrown@`, `kyle.wheatley@`). Role mailboxes (`info@`, `ceo@`, `president@`, `office@`; the list grew by `ceo`, `coo`, `cfo`, `president`, `vp`, `owner`, `founder`, `principal`, `director`), consumer domains and any other domain are dropped and the person is kept without an address. **Nothing is guessed: no address is built from a name or a pattern.** A name and an address in blocks that are not adjacent, and a byline followed by an address block, are not attributed.

**Whole tokens, whole titles, whole blocks.** An address is a COMPLETE whitespace-delimited token, trimmed only of a MATCHED wrapper pair (`(…)`, `[…]`, `<…>`, `"…"`, `'…'`, curly quotes), trailing sentence punctuation (`.,;:!?`) and a leading `mailto:`, and accepted only when `parseEmailAddress` parses all of it. An unpaired leading or trailing character is never stripped, so `'jane@peak.org` is that whole token (an apostrophe is a valid local-part character) or refused, never `jane@peak.org`, and `(jane@peak.org` is refused: `jordan.o'connor@peak.org` is that address, `a(b@peak.org` and `a@peak.org,b@peak.org` are refused, and a suffix of a token is never rescanned (never `connor@peak.org`). A title is judged as its COMPLETE local phrase, the matched title plus up to three qualifier words joined to it (`Assistant Property Manager: Jane`, `Property Manager / Leasing Agent`, and comma-separated clauses of titles either side: `Property Manager, Leasing Agent`, `Owner, Associate Broker`; a clause that is no title, such as a name, ends the scan), against the same exclusion list `rankDecisionMakerTitle` uses (#907): any excluded word refuses the person, so `Associate Broker` is refused while `Designated Broker`, `Property Manager` and `Owner and Property Manager` stand. The customer and testimonial veto reads the whole untrimmed block, not the citation trimmed from it (`Customer Review | Jane Doe, Owner` is refused).

**A testimonial section is a customer section.** The customer veto reads more than the person's own block: per page, the extractor tracks the most recent heading-like block (a short line with no sentence punctuation). A name and title that follow a testimonial or review heading (`Testimonials`, `Reviews`, `What Our Clients Say`, `Happy Customers`, ...) in separate blocks are refused until a DIFFERENT heading intervenes, and only a Title Case or ALL CAPS line that is no customer, role or person line counts as one (`Our Team` ends the section; `Happy Tenant`, `Leasing Agent` or a person's own line does not). If the block order cannot show that a different heading intervened, the person is refused. The state is per page, never carried to the next.

**A mailbox is the person's only inside their own statement.** The one-address shortcut (the block's single address is the person's) applies only when NO other named person (titled or excluded, such as `Jane Doe, Office Administrator`) appears in the block. Otherwise an address is assigned only from the person's own statement, the text from where their match starts to the next other named person or to a `|`, bullet, `·`, `©` or `;`, and must also carry the person's name as above: in `John Smith, Broker | Jane Doe, Office Administrator jane@peak.org` John has no address and Jane is not named. A block with one person and one address still assigns it, whatever the local part.

**Ranking** (`rankDecisionMakerTitle`, reordered for this PR): 0 owner; 1 president, CEO, founder, principal; 2 managing, designated, qualifying or principal broker, broker of record; 3 broker; 4 director of property management; 5 property manager (the best rank of every matching word, so `Owner / Broker` is an owner and `Principal, EVP & Qualifying Broker` a principal). Across people, **a person with a published address comes first** (the campaign needs an address), then title rank, then page order; one person named in several places is merged into the mention that carries the address. The best one becomes the company's person; a different one is never stored.

**Provider seam.** `ownSitePeople()` implements `PeopleProvider` (`providerKey` `own_site`, `maxCreditsPerLookup` 0). `findDecisionMaker` now receives `{domain, companyName, candidateId, pages}`: an HTTP source ignores `pages`. It answers `emailStatus` `published` (an address) or `unavailable` (named, none), with the citation (`source`: page URL, block id, quoted words, at most 600 characters). The domain's `acceptablePerson` still decides: title ranks, real first and last name (initials refused), own-host personal address; a person without an address is accepted as `unavailable` only with the page citation (a paid provider's answer with no address stays refused as `unverified`, with its credit). The Apollo adapter, its tests, `FSS_APOLLO_API_KEY`, `apollo-api-key`, `worker_reads_apollo_api_key`, the runbook's two-stage section and their tests are deleted; `DECISION_MAKER_TITLES` (Apollo's search filter) went with them. The interface stays, so Hunter or another provider can return later without touching acceptance or custody.

**Custody, credits, re-extraction (migration 0161).** An own-site lookup is recorded in `outreach_people_lookups` like any other (provider `own_site`, credits 0), so a company is never read again needlessly. `people_enrichment {enabled, dailyCredits}` stays: `enabled` gates the `outreach.person_enrich` pass (every ten minutes, fifty companies a pass for a free source, ten for a paid one); `dailyCredits` is a cap on credit-costing sources only, so a zero-credit reservation passes it (even at 0) and the source no longer needs a non-zero cap to be scheduled. An `own_site` row is reserved again, in place and replacing its person copy, once its thirty days pass or its candidate revision changes. A new capture of the same revision is not re-read before then. Migration 0161 is additive: `outreach_people_contacts.email` may be null, three nullable citation columns (`source_url`, `source_block_id`, `source_quote`) with one CHECK, and the 0160 person CHECK is replaced by a superset (`verified`; `published` with a citation; `unavailable` with no address), so every row 0160 admits still satisfies it. No Apollo row exists in production [INFERENCE: ADR0036's enrichment was off until a key was installed, and none was].

**A person is current only while its cited page is.** `PreparedEmailRead.pages` carry each admitted page's own deadline (its retrieval plus seven days, the capture's and its source's deadline, whichever is first); `evidenceDeadline` is the earliest of them. When an own-site person is recorded, its custody row's `expires_at` is cut to the cited page's deadline (never past thirty days), so the current-person queries (Calling, the preview, the selection, the admission) stop returning it then and the company's pages are read again. The admission proof also requires the person's cited page URL and block to be STILL among the admitted pages (else no proof) and takes `validUntil` as the earliest of the provider contact's expiry, the campaign's, `evidenceDeadline` and the cited page's own deadline. A citation to a page that is not admitted at settlement is recorded as no person.

**Skip sets are scoped to the active provider.** What an enrichment pass skips (candidates and hosts that hold custody) is read from the ACTIVE provider's rows when the source is the free `own_site`: an old paid provider's (Apollo's) custody row blocks only that provider's re-lookups and never the company's own pages being read, and the per-provider unique fences (`candidate+provider`, `host+provider`) hold an `own_site` row beside it. A paid provider is still fenced by every provider's row, and the day's credit sum and the lookups listing count all providers.

**Acceptance and email.** Admission is unchanged and still needs an address: the selection joins only persons with one, the proof and the 0160 lineage trigger join the person's address to the route, and a person without one can neither be previewed for email nor forced in. A published address is still checked by the free syntax and MX verifier (`outreach_email_verifications`) before admission; nothing bypasses it. The contact is named for the person with their title on a `research_provider` route (association 0.95 high, 0.85 medium: medium is a dash signature or an address read from a line that names the person); email 1 opens `Hi {first_name},`.

**Calling.** The prepared list read adds optional `namedPerson {name, title}` from the stored current person of the exact candidate revision and host (one query for the page's candidates), and the research card shows `Ask for <first name> — <title>`. A read of a stored row: no contact permission and no new write.

## Evidence and limits

The R1 table lists 40 of 50 hosts with a named decision-maker and 20 with a personal address on the site. Of those 20, six are another company's domain or a consumer domain (`ipisandiego.com`, `me.com`, `RiverCityMG.com`, `robbinsrealestate.com`, `snet.net`, `gmail.com`) and three are outside the captured page kinds or text (a blog post, `/owner-resources/`, and JSON-LD only), so **at most 11 of 50 are reachable by this rule** [INFERENCE from the table, not measured on live pages]. The parser is proven on 32 page shapes reconstructed from the table (`test/outreach/ownSitePeople.test.ts`): it names a decision-maker on all 29 that name one and none on the 3 that name nobody, with 9 own-domain addresses. That tests the parser, not the live yield: no page was fetched in this PR, so the real hit rate is unmeasured until the pass runs on the stored captures. The 400-record scan window of ADR0035 applies, so companies beyond the first 400 prepared ones wait for a scan cursor.

## Risks and questions for David

Names are scraped from pages: a page can name a former owner, a franchise's corporate contact or a person with the title in a story ("founded by"), and `Hi {first_name}` greets whoever the page names, so accuracy rests on the company's page; replies and bounces remain the signal. The citation (URL and quote) is kept in `outreach_people_contacts` and is erased with the contact, Firm or candidate exactly as ADR0036's person copy is (the preview lists it); the body-free custody row stays. Questions, not decided here: whether emailing a published business address of a named individual satisfies CAN-SPAM for this campaign beyond the existing footer, postal address and opt-out (the sender's duty; the page is not consent); GDPR/UK if a named person is in the EU (the campaign is US-only, residence unknown); whether a medium-confidence person (dash signature, address from a naming line) should be previewed and admitted or held for review; whether to widen the capture to keep JSON-LD and `mailto:` targets (more yield; changes retained hashes); whether the TX TREC and CA DRE licence files (R2) should later corroborate or supply a broker name, which this PR does not do; deleting the empty `apollo-api-key` entry is a destroy in the next production plan (30-day recovery window; see the runbook).
