# 0038: A named person with no published address gets ONE guessed `first@host`, kept only if a paid check says `ok`

## Status and scope

Proposed November 2 2026 for David's approval; amends [ADR0037](0037-own-site-people-extraction.md). Research #909 R3 (58 named people, 27 published and 32 guessed addresses tried at MillionVerifier) found `first@domain` won 15 of 16 verified guesses and about a third of domains are catch-all. Sending stays OFF; nothing here contacts anyone. A paid service is new spend: the worker key and the daily cap below need David's approval before they are switched on (two-stage secret, `people_enrichment.dailyCredits` above 0).

## Decision

**One guess per candidate revision.** A company whose current person came from its own pages WITHOUT an address (`unavailable`) gets `first@host` (`guessAddress`, pure): the real first name lowercased, diacritics stripped, purely `a-z` (a hyphen, space, apostrophe or a letter ASCII cannot spell refuses; nothing is repaired), at the company's OWN host, a personal mailbox that is not a role or consumer address. No other pattern is ever tried: R3's other patterns won 1 of 16 and each invalid costs a credit. **Host only:** the 0160/0161 rules bind every address to the company's host (`outreach_people_contacts` CHECK `domain=host`, `personalAddressAtHost`), and a different mail domain (R3: 4 of 19) would need a new proof the domain is the company's own (a franchisor's or vendor's role address is not); not attempted here. A host with no MX is refused free (`no_mail_domain`, nothing spent).

**Verification (`AddressVerifier`, separate from `PeopleProvider`).** `apps/worker/src/providers/millionVerifier.ts`: `GET https://api.millionverifier.com/api/v3/?api=KEY&email=ADDR&timeout=20`, one request, no retry, 30 s bound, key only in that query string (never in a message, code or log; the pass logs counts only). `result` is `ok`, `catch_all`, `unknown`, `invalid` or `disposable`; anything else is an unusable answer. A non-empty `error` (the docs show only that it is empty on success; test keys exist for no key, bad key, no credits, blocked IP, internal error) is matched loosely to `auth_failed`/`quota_exhausted`/`rate_limited`, else `unavailable`, uncharged. HTTP 401/403/402/429 are refusals (uncharged); another 4xx uncharged; a 5xx, transport failure or unreadable or unrecognised body may have been charged (`unknown`, never retried). `FSS_MILLIONVERIFIER_API_KEY` absent: no verifier, no guess, published addresses keep today's free check.

**Only `ok` makes an address.** `ok` stores a person copy with status **`verified_guess`** (migration 0162): not published by the company, provider `millionverifier`, match confidence medium (association 0.85), still citing the page the NAME was read from (so the cited-page and deadline rules apply unchanged), expiring with that page. The preview shows `verified_guess` beside `published`. `catch_all`, `unknown`, `invalid`, `disposable` leave the person named, for Calling, with no address.

**Custody and credits.** The new `outreach_people_paid_checks` ledger (migration 0162) holds one row per candidate REVISION, reserved under the setting's lock and workspace advisory lock with the daily cap re-read BEFORE the call, settled after (the ADR0036 protocol). A charged or possibly charged row is never renewed: a changed revision adds a NEW row beside the old one, preserving its credits and business date in the daily sum. Only an explicitly uncharged refusal (`failed`, 0 credits) can reuse its row after an hour. Credits by verdict (R3 balance deltas): `ok` 1, `invalid` 1, `catch_all` 0, `unknown` 0; `disposable` is undocumented and charged 1 conservatively. The daily cap sums both this ledger and legacy provider custody; 0 disables paid checks, not free own-site extraction. Every verdict is recorded. An uncertain attempt fences candidate OR host for thirty days; negative verdicts do too. A verifier failure halts guessing. Guessing takes at most ten a pass and starts nothing after a minute (one check can wait 30 s; the lease is 120 s). Cited-page expiry removes a guess's admission authority but never permits another payment for the same revision.

**Published addresses.** With a verifier and a positive daily cap, a PUBLISHED address that passed the free MX check is asked of the verifier once at admission (the same paid ledger and cap as guesses). `invalid` and `disposable` commit with an address-bound invalid check (provider `millionverifier`) and refuse admission for thirty days; `ok`, `catch_all` and `unknown` admit (the company published it; David's call). No key, disabled enrichment, a zero or exhausted cap, an already settled non-negative check, or a verifier failure in THIS call leaves the free check to decide. An existing unresolved or negative attempt is a hold, never an outage fallback; a replacement candidate on the same host cannot escape it. A guess is not checked again (the paid check already said `ok`); the free MX check and its record remain required by the 0158 trigger.

**Migration 0162** (`touches-existing` classification): creates the paid-check ledger with verdict/refusal reasons, widens the person CHECK for cited `verified_guess` addresses, and widens the verification-provider CHECK for `millionverifier`. The two widened CHECKs are dropped and re-added under their original names; neither 0160 unique constraint is dropped, replaced or widened. No stored row or routine is rewritten.

## Cost and evidence

R3: roughly half of guessed people verify `ok` (16 of 32), about 3.5 credits per ok because it also tried five more patterns; this design tries one, so per named person without an address about 0.5 ok (1 credit), 0.1 to 0.3 invalid (1 credit), the rest catch-all or unknown (0): roughly 0.6 to 0.8 credits a person. Fifty such companies cost about 30 to 40 credits; the account bought 500 (R3 used 72). The ceiling is the daily cap, nothing else. Published-address checks add at most one credit per admission (five a day). The adapter is proven against a fake HTTP (URL, parameters, bounds, no retry, no key in any output); no live call was made by this change.

## Risks and questions for David

A guessed address is not published by the company: CAN-SPAM requires the existing footer, postal address and opt-out but the sender's duty to have a lawful basis for a mailbox nobody gave us is a legal question; GDPR/UK if the person is in the EU. Questions, not decided here: whether to email guessed addresses at all, or only published ones. `ok` proves the mailbox exists, not that it is that person's (`first@` can be shared or another person's), and a catch-all domain (about a third) can never be verified, so those people stay Calling-only. Verification is not deliverability or consent. Spend needs the key installed (runbook 1.4, two stages) and a cap above zero.

**Review amendments.** (1) A paid row is an append-only attempt ledger: never renewed, one row per candidate REVISION, so every charge stays in the day's count. A guess or check that is never admitted (a `found` guess expiring with its cited page) is not re-paid for the same revision; a new revision is a new row and a new, counted charge. An UNCERTAIN attempt (`unknown`, or `reserved` in flight) is different: it fences the company for its thirty days whatever the revision or person expiry, so a revision change never pays again for a possibly charged request. The company-by-host fence is the reservation's (under the advisory lock), not a unique constraint. (2) A published address's `invalid`/`disposable` answer commits in ONE transaction with its address-bound refusal record (`outreach_email_verifications`, provider `millionverifier`); a failed record write rolls both back and the company stays fenced (`reserved`, then `unknown`), never re-asked. The settled `verdict_invalid`/`verdict_disposable` custody row is also a refusal on its own that selection (`preparedAdmissionRefusedInAdvance`) and the admission proof (`address_check_refused`) honour for thirty days, so no ordering of a crash admits a known-invalid address on the free check alone. Only a missing key, a zero cap or a verifier failure falls back to the free check.

**Closure (fences by company).** The paid fence is by candidate OR company host, checked in `reservePaidCheck` under the workspace advisory lock (the database holds only the one-row-per-revision fence; the host fence is this check): an unresolved attempt (`reserved`, `unknown`) or a settled `verdict_invalid`/`verdict_disposable` holds the company for its thirty days whatever the candidate revision, person expiry or replacement candidate on the same host. `taken` is never "no verifier answer": `checkPublishedAddress` returns `held` when the holding row is unresolved or negative, and selection (`preparedAdmissionRefusedInAdvance`) and the admission proof (`address_check_refused`, `addressRefusedByCheck`) refuse the same rows, so a rolled-back record write (custody `reserved`, then `unknown`/`interrupted` after the stale sweep) is never admitted on the free check. Only a genuine failure of THIS call (its own `unavailable`/`auth_failed`/... on its own candidate), a missing key or a zero cap falls back to the free check; a settled `ok`/`catch_all`/`unknown` answer leaves it too. A settled negative on the host also holds a later published address there for the window (a guess's `invalid` cannot be told from a published address's: custody is not keyed by address); accepted.

**Why a new table (migration 0162 rewritten before release).** The first 0162 dropped the 0160 unique constraints and replaced them with partial indexes so one custody table could hold a row per revision; the release classifier treated those unmatched constraint drops as destructive and the operator refused it. Re-adding wider keys would weaken other providers' fences. Paid checks therefore moved to their own ledger. A verified guess's person copy still hangs off a free `millionverifier` row in `outreach_people_lookups` (0 credits, renewed like `own_site`, one per candidate and per host). Its successful paid settlement and person write share a transaction; if the switch turns off before person reservation, the charge is recorded without storing a person. The daily cap sums both tables; the preview reads the guess's paid credits from its successful same-revision ledger row, not the free copy. Candidate/contact/Firm erasure removes personal copies but retains the body-free ledger and its fence; runtime has no DELETE grant. Renewing own-site custody does not itself erase an older guessed copy: its exact-revision and cited-page deadline checks still govern admission until it expires or is renewed.
