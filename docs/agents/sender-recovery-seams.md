# Sender inactivity recovery — issue #444

Approved seams: `readRampStanding`/`ensureRamp` expose persisted recovery and effective allowance; `closeSendDay`/late-signal recording expose new-day graduation and reversal; automatic admission and final outbound dispatch consume that same allowance while preserving their own accounting; existing API/outreach read contracts expose standing for the desktop. These catch persistent/concurrent decisions and controlled-provider behavior, not live received authentication or production rollout.

The activity clock uses the latest confirmed automated outbound `sent_at` or matched manual Sent message `internal_date` recorded by `direct_send_manual`/`direct_send_conversation`. Manual messages affect inactivity only. Incoming messages, jobs, health verdicts, reservations, reads and failed/uncertain submissions do not reset activity. Without recorded activity, the mailbox creation instant is a conservative durable baseline; no evidence earns progress by absence. Calendar dates use the workspace business timezone, including weekends and DST, not elapsed 24-hour blocks.

At 14 calendar dates of inactivity, atomically bind a recovery epoch to that activity anchor and start at five, bounded by the existing allowed cap. Earned healthy-day history remains independent. Each five NEW qualifying days increases the recovery stage along 5/10/15/25/35/50, bounded by historical earned stage and current lower caps. Qualifying exposure is at least80% of the actual recovery allowance, zero adverse evidence, and no unresolved dispatch; a pre-epoch or pre-stage day cannot graduate recovery. Do not manufacture traffic for exposure. Late adverse evidence removes once-only credited progress and recomputes the current recovery ceiling, including after apparent completion.

Fresh readiness derives from existing authentication, coverage, holds and unresolved provider fences. Standing does not grant sending authority: admission/final dispatch retain established release authorization, locks, window, pacing, reservations, ownership and stop checks. Missing or unknown consequential evidence stays held until its existing resolution/revalidation path; no provider guarantee, invented reputation telemetry, credential change or release activation is introduced.

Recovery completion restores the ordinary earned ramp. A mailbox already at the first earned stage still demonstrates five new qualifying days before completion; higher stages complete once the recovery ladder reaches the epoch's earned ceiling. A late reversal can reopen the epoch even after completion and after subsequent ordinary earning. Epoch and recovery-stage identity are frozen on a send day; delayed closure of a prior-stage day cannot graduate a later stage or ordinary growth after completion. Failed or sparse days remain final and never re-earn on a later healthy read.

Readiness reasons describe live mailbox evidence, not a release attestation, prospecting permission or vendor reputation guarantee.
