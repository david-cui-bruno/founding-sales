# Business Gmail sources and plain search

This map replaces the retired conversational-answering readiness inventory. It is source documentation, not production, provider, capture or sending authorization. Current operating authority remains [current state](../../../operations/current-state.md).

## Retained business Gmail paths

- Account-bound metadata qualification, correspondent rules, uncertainty review and explicit missed-message selection remain.
- Approved business originals, including original Sent replies, remain distinct from metadata observations. Quoted/forwarded material and uncertain authorship do not become verified speaker identity.
- Bounded historical import, qualified thread expansion, quota accounting, unknown-read recovery, account/generation/decision fences and coverage reporting remain.
- Attachments keep their existing metadata behavior. This deletion authorizes no new attachment-byte acquisition.
- Capture and extraction retain separate purpose, consent, data-handling, budget and capability checks. Removing Ask answering does not enable either capability.

## Search, human work and erasure

`/ask/read` retains exact SQL state, record matching, keyword passages and copied-source discovery. It does not require an answering model, canonical vector index, embedding job or model backfill. Installed desktop 1.0.51 receives its strict legacy record/task shape using the authenticated session version; current clients keep relationship details and broader task coverage.

Previously saved human tasks and annotations remain in records and Today under current source/access checks. Answer-derived creation, generated answers, sessions and private answer-history actions are removed.

Historical Ask tables, migrations, financial facts and privacy triggers remain. Explicit source deletion still redacts old copied windows, vectors, questions, titles, results and dependent action text through the existing closure. Restore cannot revive erased private content. No database drop or migration is needed.
