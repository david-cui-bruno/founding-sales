# Qualification evaluation — 5 October 2026

## Decision

Ship evidence inspection and reviewed admission with **automatic admission off**. This evaluation did not establish any automatically eligible real prospect. Zero eligible results do not establish precision. Do not activate from this report; useful autonomous yield remains unproven.

## What ran

Real public websites were fetched through the production bounded, DNS-pinned page adapter. Haiku on Amazon Bedrock selected source blocks; the domain qualification runner, reservation ledger and deterministic policy ran against an isolated schema-50 development database. No production data was mutated, no messages/calls were sent and no new search request was issued. Inputs were the existing DFW review batch and previously paid Tavily search results, not manufactured buying signals.

The first pass attempted 29 existing DFW rows: 24 produced reviewable evidence, four were unavailable and one did not create a qualification view. There were 24 model dispatches, 74 retained page observations and 96 selected facts. A separate gross reservation cap stopped further evaluation at 49 cents reserved; the recorded rounded model cost was 25 cents. The last token-count refusal was the evaluation harness's budget stop, not a Bedrock outage. No eligible result was produced.

That pass exposed a text-coverage defect: 68 of 74 observations were truncated, often because navigation occupied the first 32 blocks. Qualification now drops explicit navigation/form containers and retains up to the existing parser's 100 whole blocks; byte/text limits and hidden/testimonial filtering remain. It does not silently label truncated pages complete.

A follow-up checked four affected DFW rows plus ten existing native search-title rows: 14 rows, 11 model dispatches, 11 review results and three unavailable sources. It retained 21 observations (seven truncated) and 52 facts; 22 cents were reserved and 12 cents recorded. This is a different, selected sample, not an unbiased before/after lift measurement. Total recorded gross model cost across both passes: 37 cents; existing AWS funding accounting was used, not direct Anthropic/OpenAI billing. No new search quota was consumed.

Raw bounded observations, selected quotations, refusals and reservation summaries are stored outside the repository under the owner's local `~/.local/share/callie/qualification-evaluation/`. No credential appears in the report or artifacts.

## Source interpretation and remaining limitations

- LEAP's maintenance-coordination service breadcrumb was selected by the model as a coordination-job fact. The deterministic policy now requires explicit vacancy/hiring language before assigning the investigation tier. The regression reproduces the exact breadcrumb; a service label cannot establish a job opening.
- Herman Boswell's page supplied its company name after the text fix, but the imported display name also contains a parenthetical alias. Exact identity matching leaves it unknown. Search-result headlines likewise are often not canonical company names. Do not replace these unknowns with inferred certainty.
- Published phone numbers often occupy separate blocks from the company name. The conservative phone-association rule leaves those unresolved. This limits reviewed admission too; the existing manual firm/CSV workflow remains available.
- Source access failures remain unavailable, not evidence of absent staff or support. Marketing statements, service promises and undated generic careers content did not establish an unmet need.
- No genuine strong-hypothesis cohort of 15–20 examples was available in the existing batch. This report therefore cannot estimate recall, positive precision, conversion lift or cost per useful admitted lead. Canonical-identity/contact resolution and a genuine positive corpus need further evaluation before automatic activation.

## Independent review and repair evidence

One independent whole-branch read-only review found three material defects. All received reproducing regressions and repairs:

1. Hypothetical or resolved historical workload could become eligible. Whole-block noncurrent/conditional statements and stale dated burden now remain review-only.
2. Correction/deletion could strand a dispatched reservation after a crash. The deadline sweep now enumerates aged reservations independently of run existence/state, including while new research is disabled.
3. Every fetched page counted as corroboration. Ranking now uses supporting evidence IDs and deduplicated supporting text; unrelated pages and copies do not add priority.

The real-source text coverage and false coordination-job findings were additionally repaired. Fixture tests establish these invariants, not market usefulness.
