## Verbatim playbook

**Lifecycle:** Unreviewed → Ready → Contacted → Interviewed → Offered → Won, or Lost-Nurture (with a reason code and a re-queue date — nothing dies permanently). Every active lead carries exactly one next action with a due date; a lead without one is a bug.

**Four cadences, picked by segment.**

*Warm intros — the four PM firms, referrals, inbound DEMO texts.* Speed beats persistence: warm leads die of slowness, not pressure. Same-day reply, call to book within 24–48 hours, one nudge at day 4, graceful close at day 8. A referral left 48 hours is a wasted referral. **This is where your effort goes right now.**

*Hot — FRBO listings, community "anyone know a plumber" posts.* 8 touches over 14 days, and the first touch is within minutes of the alert (5-minute response beats 30-minute by ~100x on contact odds). Day 0 call → voicemail → instant text. Day 1 call, other time window. Day 3 text. Day 5 call + voicemail. Day 8 email. Day 11 call. Day 12 text. Day 14 break-up text.

*Cold — registry, parcel, violation leads.* 6 touches over 16 days: day 0 call/VM/text, day 2 call, day 5 text, day 9 call + VM, day 13 email, day 16 break-up.

*Post-stage cadences, where deals actually leak.* After a good interview: same-day recap text quoting their own pain back to them, book call two, pitch on day 2. After the offer: send terms day 0, nudge day 2, mutual-action check day 5 ("you said you'd try it on the Elmwood building — still good?"), value touch day 9, break-up day 12. After a Won: the 10-minute onboarding runs immediately — a yes that sits overnight cools.

**Always end with the break-up.** It out-replies every touch after the third, and it's the cheapest re-engagement setup you have.

**Volume and windows:** ~40 manual dials/day in two blocks, 8:30–10am and 4–6pm (both peak small-business answer windows), Tuesday through Thursday heaviest, Saturday mornings surprisingly good. That yields roughly 4 quality conversations a day, 15–25 a week. From a 401 or 214 local number — local presence roughly quadruples answer rates.

**Compliance rails, non-negotiable:** manual dialing only, never Callie's own number or voice for prospecting, RI hours 9–6 weekdays, MA nothing after 8pm, DNC scrubbed before first dial, opt-outs permanent and person-wide, every touch logged (the log *is* your legal defense file).

**The asks that make it compound:** research first, pitch second — you learn what they did, not what they'd do. Every completed conversation ends with "who's one other landlord I should talk to?" Every completed job ends with the referral ask plus the testimonial ask.

**Friday scoreboard, five numbers:** conversations started, offers made, paying partners, jobs completed, vendor fill rate. Kill rules: under 10% connect means fix the list; 20 conversations with zero job interest means fix the pitch before continuing; fill rate under 2–3 quotes in 24 hours means stop selling and go recruit contractors.

The honest note on sequencing: with four warm PM intros in hand, the FRBO and cold cadences should be *idling* this week. Warm intros convert at multiples of cold outreach, and you have four of them sitting untouched. The cold machine is what you run when the warm pipeline is empty — right now it isn't.
