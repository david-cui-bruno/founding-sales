# Retention, deletion and retained business history

Privacy deletion is an explicit audited workflow, not Disable, disconnect, a merge or removal of a UI panel. `packages/domain/retention/deletion.ts`, the related retention modules and source-specific lifecycle functions own the closure. Follow current approvals before irrecoverable CRM, Gmail-copy or conversation deletion.

## Originals and dependencies

A source can have original copies, attachments, transcripts, claims, commitments, task references, search passages and historical derived content. Deletion must reach the actual dependent copies and invalidate current reads/work, not just remove a visible original. Current source/version, identity/assignment and access checks remain necessary after asynchronous waits.

Removing generated Ask did not remove historical erasure obligations. The retained `crm/askAnswerLifecycle.ts` privacy lock and historical redaction paths are not answering capabilities. Old cached/copied content cannot become readable because its original feature is absent. Retained human tasks and plain keyword search still require current source access.

Gmail source deletion removes affected Callie copies and derivatives, not provider originals. Copy identity, terminal recapture barriers and deletion propagation survive reconnect/recovery. Disable stops fresh authorized work but retains history and cannot recall already accepted provider requests. Backfill and isolated diagnostics have separate acquisition scope and cleanup; they do not imply mailbox-wide deletion authority.

## Business history and suppression

The workflow removes or redacts private content while preserving required business/audit structure and minimal normalized suppression tombstones. Firm/contact/opportunity history and append-only events must not be casually deleted to make foreign keys disappear. Backup copies follow the existing retention/recovery rules; no immediate backup erasure or restore rehearsal is claimed here.

Deletion closes dependent jobs and paid attempts under the established lock order. Preserve settled or estimated charges and uncertain provider outcomes. Releasing unused reservations is not evidence that previous work was free or never happened. A later restore or import must not silently revive suppressed contact or erased source content.

## Checks and operations

Use supported preview/deletion commands and existing retention, mail, CRM and historical Ask privacy tests. Verify unavailable rereads, task/search closure, pending-job cancellation, recapture refusal, cross-workspace boundaries and actual cost conservation using disposable PostgreSQL. No data-dropping migration is required to remove a retired capability's source code.

Real deletion and restores that lose writes require explicit approval. The protected [restore runbook](runbooks/restore.md) and [suppression instructions](suppression.md) remain authoritative. This documentation change performs no deletion, restore, provider access or production acceptance.
