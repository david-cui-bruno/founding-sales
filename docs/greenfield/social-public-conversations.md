# Selected public Conversations: bounded original capture

Specification: [#928](https://github.com/david-cui-bruno/founding-sales/issues/928), part of [#783](https://github.com/david-cui-bruno/founding-sales/issues/783), roadmap4.4. **Proposed next implementation milestone; no acquisition or activation authorized.** Reader delivery [#843](https://github.com/david-cui-bruno/founding-sales/issues/843) is not acquisition. Founder publishing #925/PR927 is separate: its implementation worktree and ADR0039 were inspected read-only; this specification does not claim that work is merged, deployed or connected.

## Goal and normal journey

David can retain one explicitly selected public LinkedIn discussion and later inspect exactly what Callie observed, with its date, gaps and expiry. This is a private reference original, not an inbox, relationship assessment, buying signal, complete conversation history or permission to contact anyone. No feed discovery, monitoring, public reply, DM, invitation, model processing or automatic use in founder drafts is included.

1. In Social → Public conversations, the authenticated owner selects **Capture one public thread** and supplies the exact canonical activity URL. No background navigation occurs on typing, validation, preview or saving a selection.
2. Show the selected URL, anonymous public-web access, one observation/no refresh, text-only limits, private audience, purpose and deletion deadline. Explain that author labels are observed display names, not verified People, and that public visibility does not establish rights to retain or republish. Owner approval must expressly cover this selection and copying purpose. A missing grant leaves **Capture unavailable**, not an implicit fetch.
3. Once the released capability's provider-permission and actual-access prerequisites below are satisfied, normal authenticated selection and confirmation authorize exactly one bounded capture; no separate operator instruction is required for ordinary use. Show pending, captured, refused, failed-before-dispatch or outcome-unknown accurately. No autonomous retries. The owner may leave the page; the same opaque request identity gives status without fetching the platform again.
4. Open the retained exact ID/revision through the existing reader. Show canonical source, capture/expiry dates, anonymous access, retained message count/order, partial/unknown coverage and every attribution/time/truncation gap. Opening the retained copy never refreshes it. Opening the external source is deliberate owner navigation, not proof that its contents are still available.
5. **Remove retained copy** erases the selected original and associated acquisition bytes. Source removal can also be reported against that exact retained original. No remote deletion or account change occurs. Expiry and owner/workspace lifecycle use existing retention ownership.

## Acquisition decision and alternatives

**Selected candidate for controlled implementation: one anonymous, fresh-context public-web observation of the selected canonical activity URL.** This is not yet a supported genuine-acquisition route: applicable website copying/automation/retention permission and actual anonymous access/extraction remain unresolved. The proposed read-only adapter is not a capability of the delivered reader or publisher. Reuse the approved isolated browser host's confinement/transport machinery only after Root's integration/release; never reuse its account cookies, publication port or publication authority. No new host/provider/budget is requested. Genuine capture stays unavailable until those capability-level prerequisites are established; controlled implementation does not satisfy them.

| Option | Decision and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Anonymous public-web rendered text | Selected candidate for controlled implementation: fits ADR0030's `public_web` representation without acquiring account/private-view scope. Provider permission and actual anonymous access/layout remain unproven; public visibility is not permission. Root-only coverage can be useful after those prerequisites are met. |
| Authenticated LinkedIn browser observation | Not supported in this milestone. A public-looking logged-in page may include account-dependent material. Publishing login is not a capture grant. Login wall, challenge or account-required content is a refusal, not a reason to borrow a session. The capture adapter allows one canonical document load, at most20seconds, no pagination, expansion, scrolling, account login or follow-on profile/media navigation. Ordinary required document resources may load inside the constrained context; block downloads, media capture, trackers where separable, non-allowlisted navigation/private-network access and mutating requests. Never call hidden/internal LinkedIn interfaces to bypass unavailable rendered text. Close the isolated context and discard transient bytes on every outcome. There is no scheduled source recheck. If this cannot obtain a usable root and stable native identities, the supported result is refusal, not fabricated provenance or relaxed access.

## Authority, identity and custody

These are separate facts: **visible without login**, **selected by David**, **authorized to copy**, **authorized to read the retained copy**, and **authorized to publish/send**. None implies the next. Generic LinkedIn browser-platform permission is settled; this design does not ask to reapprove it. It also does not supply a concrete capture grant. ADR0004/0009/0010 preserve acquisition/processing/action separation; existing Gmail diagnostic approvals do not authorize LinkedIn acquisition.

No genuine URL or exact capture approval was supplied in #843/#783/#925 or this assignment. Therefore **the live selected-original identity is unresolved**. Once the capability-level prerequisites are satisfied, David's normal signed product selection supplies the canonical URL `https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:<digits>/`, copying purpose and deadline, and confirmation grants one anonymous observation for private reference within the disclosed bounds. Retention is no later than30days from capture or any earlier source restriction. This is normal product consent, not a per-run operator review. A genuine-source engineering acceptance exercise is different: its separately authorized bounded instruction identifies environment, read allowance, stop/cleanup conditions and whether its copy may enter production. Fixtures cannot supply either genuine consent or evidence of platform permission. Signed-in-only content requires a changed access-scope decision, not a fallback.

Before external work, allocate an opaque request ID and immutable destination `threadId`, bind owner/workspace, exact URL, purpose/access mode, approved deadline and current grant revision. Reserve dispatch once durably; recheck current owner/principal/grant after waits and immediately before dispatch and retained publication. Pause/revocation wins against new dispatch/commit; already-issued observation remains recorded as used/unknown without copied content after revocation. A lost response or worker restart uses the same request, never a second navigation. A new request is not an automatic retry or refresh. Erased requests are terminal and cannot be replayed.

Adapter-owned observation time is the actual render/extraction time, not the user's declaration or database save time. Canonical URL must match the selected activity identity after navigation; reject a different activity, redirect to feed/profile/login, unsupported host/path or ambiguous root. Do not derive a URL from names, guess IDs, use URL shorteners or permit arbitrary server-side URL fetching. The request retains no credentials. Captured provenance binds the request to final canonical identity, anonymous access, parser version, capture time and retained revision. Store no duplicate HTML, screenshots, response dumps or prose audit history.

The existing contracts bound snapshots to50 messages/64,000serialized UTF-8 bytes, each text to2,000characters/8,000bytes, and author labels to200characters. Keep those bounds. Root is first; include only already-rendered messages with stable native message identity, in observed order. Missing/unidentifiable comments are omitted with coverage gaps; no locally invented native IDs. Parent identity can be retained or explicitly unavailable. Native activity identity supports the root; an adapter must not label an excerpt/repost as the original author without observed attribution. If the root is absent or unidentifiable, refuse rather than retaining comments as that original.

Text is the observed rendered passage, not a summary or reconstruction. Hashes establish local retained-byte integrity only, not platform authenticity, rights or complete coverage. Display names remain **Observed author**, not verified Person/profile ownership. Set author unknown if absent/ambiguous. Only an explicitly observed unambiguous absolute instant becomes a known publication time; relative labels, activity-ID arithmetic and inferred timezone yield unknown. `textCoverage=complete` means the individual rendered message was visibly uncollapsed and fully retained, never that the thread is complete; ellipsis/collapse or local size cutoff is truncated, unresolved layout is unknown. Stop at bounds and preserve explicit partial coverage rather than silently dropping middle text. Thread coverage is always partial or unknown, never complete; count is retained messages, not all platform comments.

## Lifecycle, access, privacy and refusal

- Preserve owner-private admin/member checks, exact revision reads, current-principal-after-wait checks and late-response/browser-memory clearing from the delivered reader. A public source is not workspace-public retained content. No new search/Ask/CRM projections or model egress.
- Expiry is `min(actual capturedAt + 30days, approved deadline, known source deadline)`. Editing approval, queue delays or saving cannot restart that clock. Unknown source expiry is disclosed, not asserted absent. More restrictive known source terms refuse capture or shorten the horizon; this software maximum is not a platform license.
- No refresh or background source-loss detection is claimed. The UI states **Current source availability not checked**. A timeout/denial is not proof of deletion. An explicit owner removal or supported observation establishing removal/restriction invokes exact canonical-source erasure across this owner's retained copies. Do not add a monitoring fetch to obtain that evidence. A merely edited remote post does not silently rewrite an immutable snapshot.
- Existing retention erases URL, payload, message/locator/author identities, hashes and coverage; keep only opaque denial identity/revision/timing/reason. Acquisition selection/custody records must join that erasure, with no URL/hash/prose retained in a second ledger. Existing opaque IDs prevent replay of the erased request, not universal recognition of that URL forever. There is no ordinary reimport; any future distinct recapture is outside this milestone and requires explicit renewed scope, never an automatic workaround for erasure. Owner departure/role loss, workspace deletion and expiry also cover pending/in-flight results and transient buffers.
- Signed **Remove retained copy** uses the existing retention owner, not a new deletion store. Fence deletion with publication so an in-flight result cannot resurrect erased bytes; stale list/read/capture responses clear rather than repaint. No source bytes in logs, telemetry, error reports, URLs/query parameters, browser persistence, receipts or operator reports. Render text as inert text; no active embedded content or attachment downloads.
- Refuse unsupported visibility, private/group/DM content, insufficient authority, rights/privacy concerns identified by the owner, login/challenge, changed canonical identity, missing root/native IDs, unbounded/unsupported layout or unsafe network target. Explain the actionable category without leaking source prose. Rate limits/warnings stop; no bypass, alternate account or paid service. Failures before dispatch may be retried only by a fresh deliberate owner command; unknown dispatch consumes the observation allowance and has status/cleanup only. No blind retry or success-from-empty result.

Internally own dispatch-once custody, extraction, lifecycle and retention handoff; callers never fabricate a `SocialPublicThreadRetainInput`. Add a capture-specific read-only adapter seam because platform acquisition and controlled fixtures actually vary. Do not extend `SocialPublisherPort` with arbitrary browsing or expose CDP/session secrets. Reuse normal signed command authentication and retained-principal/transaction fences, Social retention and owner lifecycle; no parallel auth/queue/store/daemon. Root chooses contiguous schema changes during implementation, not a guessed number here.

Official public documentation reviewed November4,2026:

- LinkedIn's [post visibility guide](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a523141) describes **Anyone** as visible on or off LinkedIn; its [off-platform sharing guide](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a523372/sharing-public-posts-on-and-off-linkedin?lang=en) explains that public-profile settings can still limit signed-out access. A public label/URL is not proof this context can read all comments. These guides do not guarantee DOM stability, complete replies or anonymous metadata.
- [Getting API access](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/authentication/getting-access) lists open `w_member_social` for writing, not thread reading. [Posts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/posts-api) documents authenticated known-ID retrieval, restricted `r_member_social` and role-dependent organization reads. [Comments](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/shares/comments-api) separately documents restricted `r_member_social_feed` and role-dependent `r_organization_social_feed`; do not conflate those scopes. [Community Management access](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/community-management-api-migration-guide) requires product review; this repository has no evidence here of the necessary approved read entitlements. Canonical browser activity URLs do not establish universal share/UGC conversion or an API credential.
- [Marketing API storage requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/data-storage-requirements) specify48hours for member social activity and24hours for other-member profile cache, with the shortest overlapping restriction prevailing. These API rules neither grant browser collection nor endorse30-day browser retention. [API Terms §§4.1–4.5](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/l/api-terms-of-use) distinguish storage permission, service necessity and deletion obligations; §§3.1(24)–(25) also constrain mixed API/non-API use within their applicable scope. No API/browser fallback or retention license is inferred here. Owner consent does not override applicable provider restrictions.
- [Prohibited software](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387/prohibition-of-scraping-software?lang=en) expressly addresses third-party scraping/copying and website automation. The [User Agreement §§1.2,8.2(2),(4),(6),(13)](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement) applies to visitors as well as members and restricts automated copying, unauthorized access methods and use of others' content; §8 permits exceptions explicitly authorized by LinkedIn in separate writing. Anonymous access is not an exemption. The existing accepted browser-platform permission is preserved, not reopened, but the cited publication research does not establish that its scope covers copying and retaining other members' discussions. Root must resolve that applicability against the already accepted grant and applicable source restrictions before enabling this candidate for genuine acquisition. Public visibility, David's consent and a30-day software maximum cannot substitute for that scope. This is a capability-level prerequisite, not a new permission interview or per-capture proof packet.
