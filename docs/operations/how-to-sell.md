# How to sell with Callie

Start with the [canonical journey map](../features/README.md) and [current operating state](current-state.md). This is a daily navigation guide, not a deployment or activation ledger. Browser is primary; the installed desktop remains fallback. Do not reconnect accounts, import history or contact prospects to test navigation.

## Daily flow

1. **Today → Prospects:** review due work, open the exact retained prospect and its sourced context. Raw observations stay workable with unknown phones or qualification. Prepared research is not confirmed customer need. The [supply journey](../features/selling.md#supply-and-prospects) distinguishes retained originals, discovery and canonical Firm creation.
2. **Calling:** check the exact selected subject, phone provenance and current eligibility verdicts before any separately authorized call. Browser calling is preferred where configured; unavailable device/provider or phone fallback is honest, not a reason to bypass a refusal. Real audio, microphone and recording authority are separate from local fake-device acceptance.
3. **Record only what happened:** use the outcome form only after actual activity, or explicitly fictional activity in the isolated local harness. Private notes are not recipient-facing email. A promised email uses independently authored content and one shared private draft in **Email**. Check the exact receipt and readback. UNKNOWN means reconcile the same identity, not save again with a new command.
4. **Email:** review promised follow-ups and incoming replies. Approved eligible outbound automation is the target, not routine batch approval. Deployment of campaign/opening source is not permission to enable sending. Preserve approved offer/content, source authority, cap5/lower ramps, local weekday windows and all reply/refusal/suppression/unknown-write stops. Incoming replies remain manual. Existing regular **Sequences** in desktop have their own enrollment prerequisites; do not enroll twice after an outcome already started one.
5. **Social:** use private voice preferences and explicit edits for private drafts. Approved direction includes supported automatic public publishing, but current adapter/account/source/route and data/model/budget authority must exist first. Public Conversations are not DMs. Candidate pages and outgoing posts are not retained public-thread originals. Manual LinkedIn activity recording does not authorize an external LinkedIn action.
6. Return to **Today** for the next valid action. A callback intention displayed in history is not proof of reminder execution. A booking link is not a scheduled meeting.

## Before any real effect

Stored owner OFF and newer holds remain binding. An office endpoint does not prove identity, DNC clearance or channel permission. Preserve personal-cell refusal, hours/state rules, shared four attempts/14 days and current covered-code registry requirements. The iPhone lane is closed; historical GOs are consumed, not reusable permissions.

For Gmail capture, extraction, CRM corrections/deletion, Ask, meetings, devices and settings, use [relationships and controls](../features/relationships-and-controls.md). Existing Gmail scope is incoming+Sent business-only new originals, no history, Haiku4.5 and $1/day/$20/month. Empty captured bodies are not originals or useful-model proof. Do not reconnect, backfill, raise caps or change consent merely to make a journey pass.

[Verification instructions](../features/verification.md) separate retained local acceptance, hosted reads, persisted writes and provider effects. Current gaps belong in [PLAN](../../apps/web/PLAN.md), not a new blanket requalification campaign.
