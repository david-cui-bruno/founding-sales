## The daily flow

1. **Today:** Review overdue replies and callbacks first. Select a firm from the queue, read its prepared brief and verify the details. **Firms → All firms** is the directory; **Pipeline** tracks opportunities. A prepared brief is research, not confirmed customer need. Research can incur provider charges, so wait for the funding check before requesting new runs.
2. **Call:** Use the selected firm's **Call** control only when enabled. Calling-hour and other refusals are deliberate stops. Read the recording disclosure shown in the call panel. Do not bypass a disabled button or call prospects just to test the tool.
3. **Record the actual outcome:** Open **Notes and outcome**, choose **Which task** and **What happened**, add your note and select **Record**. Logging an external call is history, not proof that Callie placed it.
4. **Agree on email follow-up:** After a reached conversation, choose **No follow-up**, **One approved e-mail**, or **An agreed sequence**. Review the person, messages and dates. Recording an agreed sequence attempts to start it immediately: check the **started/refused** notice. This records their actual agreement, not automated email admission.
5. **Regular sequences:** **Sequences** contains published schedules and templates. Use firm-page **Sequences → Enrol** only when appropriate, not after a sequence already started. It needs an active contact and open Pipeline opportunity. Eligible emails send automatically under pause, caps, ramp, timing, suppression and stops. Sequence calls appear in **Today** for you to make manually. A phone number alone is not enrollment permission.
6. **Replies:** Review incoming replies promptly. Replies, refusals, opt-outs, suppression and closed opportunities stop obsolete outreach. Record the stop and do not continue on another channel. Return to **Today** for the next valid action.
