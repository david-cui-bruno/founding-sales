3. **Record the actual outcome:** Open **Notes and outcome**, choose **Which task** and **What happened**, add your note and select **Record**. Logging an external call is history, not proof Callie placed it.
4. **Agree on follow-up:** Choose **No follow-up**, **One approved e-mail**, or **An agreed sequence**, reflecting the actual agreement. 6. **Replies:** Review promptly. Replies, refusals, opt-outs, suppression and closed opportunities stop obsolete outreach. Record the stop, do not continue on another channel, return to Today.
