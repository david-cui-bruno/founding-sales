# Automatic email worker

Implemented by #421 on the #420 atomic admission path. Source work is not a production release or activation receipt. Normal API activation remains unavailable and the database retains `CHECK(NOT enabled)`. Representative exact-version evaluation and actual sender/authentication verification remain required by #423–#425. Calls and routine reply automation remain off.

The ordinary scheduler registers a database-only `outreach.email_admit` bulk job. It considers enabled controls at most hourly, visits at most 25 workspaces per pass, and refuses another batch while one is queued, retryable or running. The key includes workspace (queue uniqueness), control revision and UTC hour. Unexpected worker failures use the existing job lease/fence machinery with three attempts; no research provider or reservation is part of this path.

The worker selects each candidate's latest qualification at the current candidate revision and policy/prompt. Call-review states are included. It reads at most 501 current, owed runs: a pool above 500 refuses the whole batch rather than ranking a truncated page ahead of unseen stronger prospects. This ceiling is an operational refusal, not a change to targeting. Resolve oversized pools before activation; do not remove the bound or use a quota-filling fallback.

Stored evidence is evaluated with the email policy at database time. Unsupported/contrary evidence is deferred without purchasing more research. Supported prospects use the existing comparator: help request, operational burden, investigation, fit-only; then corroborating source URLs, recency, explicitly supported named contact, firm name and candidate ID. Unknown/expired event dates become fit-only. Office contacts remain Office. Ranking occurs before the batch's maximum 25 atomic attempts and live mailbox capacity checks.

Every admission reacquires the #420 authority checks in the same transaction: workspace stop gate, current evaluated controls, active owner/mailbox, approved reusable sequence, current run, identity, ownership, stops and complete fresh first-party evidence. Normal admission creates the attributed route, email-first plan and reusable-sequence enrollment atomically. Route validation remains separate; creation does not grant technical sendability.

The ramp and mailbox day ledger account for actual claimed sends and already queued commitments. Admission neither reserves provider capacity nor advances healthy-day ramp exposure. Capacity is a ceiling, never a target. Capacity exhaustion stops the batch and rechecks just after the next workspace business-day reset (00:05 local); sender health and prospect holds recheck hourly. A run/control revision permits at most seven refusal checks. Success, permanent uncertainty and exhausted rechecks have no next wake; changed evidence or control revision earns a fresh evaluation. No recipient review is required.

The existing sender independently rechecks authorization, route validation, stops, unsettled attempts, ramp cap and pacing at dispatch. New enrollments preserve the five touches at calendar offsets 0/3/7/13/20, weekday 09:00–11:00 recipient-local window/default 10:00, and coordinated channel limits. Reply and booking stop cold enrollments; an opt-out suppresses sending immediately through the normal suppression path. Original Shirley enrollment is untouched. Discovery scheduling/budgets and read-only monitors are unchanged.
