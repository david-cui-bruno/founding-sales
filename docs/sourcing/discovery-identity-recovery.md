# Supported discovery identity recovery

Qualification policy `qualification-v3` offers only complete first-party observations to extraction. All fetched observations remain in the original run history. Answers citing a retained but excluded page are refused; already incurred provider cost remains settled. A run queued under another qualification version stops before fetching or counting tokens. The prompt remains `qualification-growth-v6`, and email fit remains `outreach-email-fit-v2`.

After a successful current-version qualification, the worker can correct an untouched discovery candidate using one literal legal company label in a supported office contact card. It checks all published cards for competing identities, including addresses the model did not select. The model must still select the same supported business email; residential management and current geography must pass the existing email-fit predicates. It does not invent a name, email, pain signal or domain relationship.

Recovery uses the existing correction command under the send gate and candidate/evidence locks. Only revision-one, unblocked, needs-review candidates created with their original hit in the same completed discovery transaction, matching source/query and automated preparation metadata, qualify. A hit later attached to an existing candidate is insufficient; previously retained hits whose origin cannot meet this proof defer conservatively. Manual staging, edited candidates, admitted/outreach-linked candidates and identity collisions remain protected. Original discovery attribution, observations and runs remain intact; the correction is audited and increments the candidate revision.

The old revision cannot authorize admission. A fresh qualification is requested within ordinary research controls. If the quota defers that request, the supported correction remains and ordinary scheduling can retry later; there is no cap override, forced discovery or extra network request in the correction transaction. Research settings and holds are read after acquiring the send gate, so a hold committed while recovery waits is seen before correction. Research pauses prevent recovery. Interpretation reuse also applies these guards and cannot reuse facts from incomplete pages.
