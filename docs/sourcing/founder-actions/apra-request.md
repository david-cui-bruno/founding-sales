# APRA Request — RI Rental Registry Bulk Extract (RIDOH)

## Before you send

**Where to send (verified from health.ri.gov, updated Jan 2026):**

- **Preferred:** RIDOH's NextRequest online portal — <https://healthandhumanservicesdepts-ri.nextrequest.com/>. Lets you track progress and receive records electronically.
- **Email:** pamela.lopes@health.ri.gov (RIDOH public records contact, Pamela Lopes, 401-222-1036)
- **Mail:** Rhode Island Department of Health, ATTN: Pamela Lopes, Suite 404, Three Capitol Hill, Providence, RI 02908
- **Fax:** 401-222-1797

**What to expect:**

- RIDOH has **10 business days** to respond, extendable another 20 business days for good cause (R.I. Gen. Laws § 38-2-3(e), § 38-2-7).
- Posted fees: $0.15/page for copies, up to $15/hour search-and-retrieval with the **first hour free**. Electronic extracts are charged at actual production cost. RIDOH Legal Services provides a fee estimate before proceeding, which the letter below requests explicitly.
- Some fields may be redacted under APRA exemptions (§ 38-2-2(4)). Landlord names, business addresses, and lead certificate status are already published in the registry's public search tool, so a bulk extract of those fields is a reasonable request. Personal email addresses and phone numbers may be withheld.
- If denied, you can appeal to the RIDOH Director, then the RI Attorney General, then Superior Court. The letter does not need to state a purpose, and you are not required to identify yourself (§ 38-2-3(j)).

Copy the letter below into the portal's description field or send as a PDF attachment. Fill in the bracketed placeholders first.

Pursuant to the Rhode Island Access to Public Records Act, R.I. Gen. Laws § 38-2-1 et seq., I request a copy of records from the statewide Rental Registry established under R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-18-58 and administered by the Department of Health.

Specifically, I request a bulk extract of the current registry containing, for each registered rental property:

1. Property address (street, city/town, ZIP);
2. Owner/landlord name(s), including any business entity responsible for leasing;
3. Owner/landlord mailing address of record;
4. Property manager of record, if any;
5. Number of rental units;
6. Lead certificate status and certificate expiration date, where applicable;
7. Date of registration.

I request these records in a machine-readable electronic format, with CSV preferred (Excel or another structured export is acceptable). Under § 38-2-3(k), records maintained electronically should be provided electronically where reasonably possible.

If any portion of the requested data is exempt from disclosure under § 38-2-2, I ask that you release the remaining non-exempt fields and identify the specific exemption relied upon for anything withheld.

I am willing to pay reasonable costs as permitted by § 38-2-4. **Please provide a written fee estimate before incurring any charges**, so I may confirm or narrow the request.

Additionally, please advise whether the Department offers any standing or recurring-request arrangement (for example, a quarterly refresh of the same extract), or whether a periodic export of the registry is already published, so that I can avoid submitting duplicative requests.

I note that § 38-2-3(e) provides ten (10) business days for a response. Please send the records and any correspondence to the email address below.

Turnstile), and the open-data portal's permit dataset ends in 2018. A public-records
request to the City of Providence is the clean path.

**Where:** City of Providence public records portal (city clerk / APRA request —
providenceri.gov, "Public Records Request", also NextRequest-based) directed to the
Department of Inspection and Standards (DIS).

**Ask for:** all building/mechanical/electrical/plumbing permit records from
[DATE − 12 months] to present, and ideally a recurring monthly export: permit number,
type, status, application and issue dates, property address, parcel/plat-lot, owner
name, declared value. CSV preferred. Same fee-estimate-first language as above.
