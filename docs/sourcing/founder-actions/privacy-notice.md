Callie builds software for rental property owners. To tell property owners about it, we maintain a small outreach database. This notice explains what is in it, where it comes from, and your choices. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

## What we collect

Business contact information about rental property owners and managers:

- Name (individual or business entity)
- Mailing address of record
- Property and parcel information (address, unit count, registration and lead certificate status)
- Phone number
- Email address

That is the entire list. We do not collect or infer anything sensitive: no race, ethnicity, religion, health, financial account, biometric, precise location, or similar data, about anyone, ever.

## Where it comes from

- **Government records:** the Rhode Island Rental Registry (maintained by the RI Department of Health under R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-18-58), municipal property tax rolls, and other public records.
- **Public listings:** publicly listed business contact information, and contact-lookup services that work from public and commercially available records.

We do not buy data about consumers, scrape private accounts, or collect anything from people who visit our website beyond what a separate website notice covers.

## Why we collect it

One purpose: to contact rental property owners about Callie's property-management software, by phone, mail, or email. We do not use this data for advertising profiles, credit or tenant screening, or any eligibility decision of any kind.

## How long we keep it

- If we have not been able to reach you and you have not become a customer, we delete your record after **12 months**.
- If you ask us not to contact you, we keep only the minimum needed (for example, your phone number) on a permanent **suppression list** so we never contact you again. That entry is never deleted, because deleting it would risk contacting you again.

## We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share this data with anyone for money or anything else of value. Not now, not ever. It is used only by Callie to reach out to you.

## Your choices

- **Stop all contact:** tell us once, by any channel, and we add you to the permanent suppression list. Effective immediately.
- **Deletion:** ask us to delete your record and we will, keeping only the suppression entry if you also want no further contact.
- **Correction:** if something we have is wrong (for example, you sold the property), tell us and we will fix or delete it.

No forms, no verification maze. An email or a sentence on a phone call is enough.
