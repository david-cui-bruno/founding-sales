## Policy requirements

1. **Permission is not readiness.** Treat David's stated permission from LinkedIn as platform/account-use permission for the defined broad LinkedIn automation. It does not prove product implementation, supported LinkedIn/provider access, account authentication, safe posting/DM operation, deployed configuration, or activation. Keep those evidence gates distinct. Existing feasibility research says OAuth/readback is unverified and retains native/manual fallback; current-state release hold and scoped Enable checks remain in force (`docs/sourcing/linkedin-automation-architecture-research.md:3-5,13-16`; `docs/operations/current-state.md:15-18,53-55`; `docs/sourcing/roadmap-20261007.md:17-20,37-41`).
2. **Target only a supported fit.** A relevant target is a property-management firm/person supported by selected, current evidence and the approved targeting policy, not a search result or inferred profile association. Preserve independent Person, firm-specific Contact, Firm relationship and Endpoint/Route distinctions (`GLOSSARY.md:7-29`). Do not infer identity, employment, portfolio, need, or relationship from name/domain similarity. Existing email discovery has complete-source identity/geography and email-specific same-domain/office-route checks. Preserve those for email, but do not mechanically require an email-domain match for a LinkedIn endpoint. A separate supported LinkedIn person/firm/profile-association contract must be chosen; no guessing identities or loosening targeting to force yield (`docs/sourcing/funnel-discovery-research-602-20261013.md:48-57`; `docs/sourcing/discovery-identity-recovery.md:3-9`).
3. **One bounded introduction DM.** Permit at most one initial, individually targeted introduction per supported person/firm association, scoped to David’s authorized LinkedIn account. Message content must be truthful, evidence-grounded and relevant; do not imply prior relationship, referral, need, consent, or a guaranteed outcome. Enforce owner/account scope at enqueue and final dispatch; retain immutable content/target/account binding to the approved standing policy and fresh association/suppression checks, without requiring individual recipient review. No autonomous multi-step sales sequence follows this introductory send. The exact approval/UI and permissible copy remain implementation/owner choices, not established by the available contracts.
4. **Credibility via useful posts, independently controlled.** Useful public posting may support credibility but is not evidence that a recipient saw, trusted, or responded to a post. Reuse the approved LinkedIn native posting boundary: frozen account/content/time approval, exact account/content check, durable pre-submit marker, inspect-after-attempt, and never treat a click as success; unknown submission is inspected, not retried (`apps/desktop/src/main/social/adapters/linkedinAdapter.ts:20-32,36-55,58-86`; `docs/sourcing/linkedin-automation-architecture-research.md:23-30`). Keep post acceptance separate from DM permission and funnel conversion.
5. **Quota is global, not per feature.** A shared owner/account quota must count automated and manual LinkedIn actions, including invitations/connections, DMs, follow-ups (which this policy otherwise disallows), and posts, with action-class limits, rolling windows, ramp/lower-cap behavior and platform restriction cooldowns. Reserve atomically before external action; manual activity must be represented or conservatively reduce available capacity. The exact limits/window and whether posts share a numeric budget are human policy decisions. Do not invent platform “safe” quotas: the existing research explicitly rejects vendor-estimated invitation limits as official constraints (`docs/sourcing/linkedin-automation-architecture-research.md:44-48`; `docs/sourcing/roadmap-20261007.md:39-41`).
6. **Duplicates and uncertain actions fail closed.** Deduplicate by owner, LinkedIn account, stable person identity, firm association and action purpose; a changed or ambiguous identity blocks. Before a retry, reconcile durable attempt/receipt state. A timeout, lost response, uncertain click, account switch, stale approval or incomplete readback means unknown/held, not success or a new send. This follows the existing social adapter’s attempted-once and unknown-inspection behavior (`linkedinAdapter.ts:28-32,48-55,58-75`) and the broader outbound uncertain-attempt fence (`docs/sourcing/funnel-email-readiness-research-603-20261013.md:34-38`).
7. **Reply and opt-out are hard stops.** Any inbound reply stops all queued invitations/DMs and pending follow-up for that person; explicit opt-out suppresses that person/route and, when scope is ambiguous, the firm/account association until David resolves it. Preserve suppression across manual and automated paths; no automated acknowledgement or rearm. An opt-out is not permission to send a “confirmation.” Broader stop events (account restriction, sender/auth failure, identity mismatch, booking, stale evidence, duplicate or uncertain attempt) also hold work. Existing policy keeps automatic admission and routine replies off, preserves holds and says the owner handles substantive replies/calls (`docs/operations/current-state.md:53-55`; `HANDOFF-2026-10-07.md:11,35-39`; `docs/sourcing/funnel-email-readiness-research-603-20261013.md:38-39`).
8. **Visible handoff, not autonomous sales.** Surface every substantive reply as a David-owned, actionable Today item with source/context, reply status, suppression/hold, and a business-day deadline; opening/review does not silently send or clear ambiguity. David owns substantive sales conversation and calls. Booking logistics can be delegated only as a separately authorized narrow exception (e.g., offer the already-approved booking link or coordinate a chosen slot); it must not answer qualification, negotiate, promise availability, or autonomously call. The existence of an approved Cal.com link in historical handoff is dated evidence, not a fresh booking-capacity/provider proof (`HANDOFF-2026-10-07.md:1-3,11,37-39`; `docs/sourcing/funnel-email-readiness-research-603-20261013.md:7,38-39`; `docs/sourcing/roadmap-20261007.md:5-7`). Broad routine replies and autonomous calls remain OFF. No response, booking, meeting, or qualified-conversation guarantee.

## Minimal end-to-end test / stop matrix

| Scenario | Expected observable outcome | Stop / retry rule |
|---|---|---|
| Supported target, approved account, below shared cap | Exactly one targeted intro becomes an attributable durable attempt | No second intro for same identity/purpose |
| Unsupported or ambiguous person↔firm association, stale evidence, wrong owner/account | No dispatch; reason visible for David | Hold until supported human correction; never infer/auto-repair association |
| Manual actions consume remaining global cap | Automated action is deferred/refused at same shared quota boundary | No per-channel quota bypass |
| Provider accepted but response lost / click outcome uncertain | Attempt remains unknown; inspect exact account/recipient/action receipt | Never blind retry or report sent absent complete evidence |
| Reply arrives before queued action dispatch | Pending actions suppressed/held; substantive reply appears in David’s work queue | Do not auto-respond; human handles sales content |
| Explicit opt-out, including race with dispatch | Durable scoped suppression wins; no subsequent action | Preserve suppression; no acknowledgement/rearm |
| Narrow booking logistics selected | Only approved booking logistics text/action is available under separate scope | Any sales substance, unclear intent, capacity uncertainty or request for a call returns to David |
| Useful post published or scheduled | Exact post/account status is visible independently | Does not authorize DM, prove engagement, or permit retry after unknown outcome |

Acceptance is an observable real workflow, not a synthetic pass alone: demonstrate the selected target’s evidence/association and scoped account, enforce combined manual+automated cap, one durable DM attempt, reply/opt-out race stops, useful post status, and visible David handoff; separately prove provider behavior and deployment/activation readiness. Do not claim representative conversion or guarantee responses from a small/controlled sample (`GLOSSARY.md:83-89`; `docs/sourcing/roadmap-20261007.md:20,35`).

## Proposed implementation slices and dependencies

1. **Affected policy/contract completion:** finish only the unresolved standing LinkedIn targeting/content/cadence/logistics choices against the canonical agreement and existing acceptance contracts. This gates live actions and implementation whose behavior depends on those choices, not all source preparation or unrelated capabilities. This note alone is not authority to activate.
2. **Shared target + quota + suppression domain contract:** scoped stable identity/firm association, manual-activity accounting, dedupe, atomic reservations, stop/unknown states. Depends on slice 1; should be transport-neutral and reuse outbound safeguards.
3. **LinkedIn adapter/provider proof:** account identity, supported authorized mechanism, durable attempt and readback/unknown recovery for one DM; native post remains its own acceptance. Depends on slice 1 and shared contract; no live account operation in this research.
4. **Human workflow and narrow booking exception:** David-visible substantive reply/handoff; separately gated logistics-only message capability, if approved. Depends on stop/suppression contract and explicit human decision; routine replies/calls remain disabled.
5. **Integrated acceptance:** exercise the matrix across manual+automated quota, races, duplicates, provider uncertainty, post status and handoff, then satisfy existing release/Enable gates. Depends on slices 2–4 and actual provider/configuration proof; controlled tests do not equal activation.
